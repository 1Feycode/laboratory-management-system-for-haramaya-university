# 11. Stakeholder Interview Questionnaire

This interview instrument is designed for field requirements validation across administrative and academic departments at Haramaya University. Because empirical operational workflows vary between colleges, these questions target specific procedural realities that cannot be confirmed from public documentation alone.

---

## 1. Group A: ULMD Leadership & Unit Supervisors (Central Lab Office 34)

### Current Asset Tracking & Numbering
1. What official coding or asset registration numbering system is currently mandated by Haramaya University for laboratory equipment versus general university fixed property?
2. Are laboratory instruments officially tagged with physical barcode or metal plate labels, or are records maintained exclusively on paper ledgers?
3. What is the official threshold (e.g., monetary value, expected lifespan) that defines a capital laboratory asset versus a consumable tool?

### Inter-College Transfers & Governance
4. When equipment is transferred from a college laboratory (e.g., HiT or CAES) to the Central Laboratories Unit for shared research use, what physical clearance forms are executed?
5. How does ULMD resolve custody disputes when an instrument purchased by a specific research grant is requested for undergraduate teaching?

### Condemnation and Disposal
6. What is the statutory procedure under Haramaya University Senate Legislation for formally condemning damaged or obsolete laboratory equipment and writing it off the university balance sheet?
7. How long must historical maintenance and asset records be preserved after an instrument is retired or scrapped?
8. Does an approved condemnation release the manufacturer/serial-number pair for a future asset record, or must that pair remain reserved permanently?
9. What exact institutional identifier or document is the condemnation reference? Who issues or approves it, what evidence must be retained, and which records-retention rule applies?

### Asset Category Governance
10. Which existing system roles may create, edit, delete, activate, or deactivate asset categories? Should category changes be restricted to ULMD, or should other roles be able to submit requests?
11. Should categories have an active/inactive lifecycle so an unused category can be retired without deleting historical references?

## 2. Group B: Laboratory Technology Unit (Engineers & Maintenance Specialists)

### Maintenance Workflow & Communication
1. How does your unit receive repair requests today (paper memos, telephone calls, in-person visits to Central Lab Office 34, or informal messages)?
2. What is the average time between when a laboratory instrument breaks down in a department and when your unit receives formal notification?
3. What are the most common causes of prolonged equipment downtime (e.g., lack of diagnostic schematics, availability of imported replacement parts, administrative purchase approval delays)?

### Preventive Maintenance & Calibration
4. Does the unit follow standardized preventive maintenance schedules (PMS) for high-value instruments, or is maintenance primarily reactive ("break-fix")?
5. What certified standards, buffers, or reference tools are available in-house for calibrating precision balances, spectrophotometers, and autoclaves?
6. When an external vendor or manufacturer bioengineer performs maintenance, how are service reports and warranties captured and archived?

### Facility Interface
7. Where does the boundary lie between your unit’s repair responsibilities and those of EFMD’s Building and Machineries Operations and Maintenance (BMOM) unit regarding laboratory electrical and plumbing systems?

## 3. Group C: Department Laboratory Managers & Technical Assistants (TAs)

### Daily Custody & Logbooks
1. What logbooks or documentation do you maintain inside your physical laboratory room on a daily basis (equipment user logs, breakdown logs, chemical inventory sheets)?
2. How do you handle informal borrowing of portable testing equipment (e.g., multimeters, pH meters, pipettes) between departments or colleagues?
3. What happens when a laboratory technician leaves or is reassigned—how is asset custody formally handed over to the successor?

### Incident Reporting & Verifications
4. When a student or researcher damages an instrument, what incident report is generated, and who authorizes the repair request?
5. After the ULMD Technology Unit repairs an instrument, does the laboratory manager perform a formal operational test run before signing off on the repair?
6. What are your primary frustrations with the current laboratory maintenance process at Haramaya University?
7. For equipment shared by researchers from several departments, which laboratory and room remain accountable for custody? Is a time-bounded borrowing/access record sufficient, or must the asset have multiple simultaneous locations or custodians?
