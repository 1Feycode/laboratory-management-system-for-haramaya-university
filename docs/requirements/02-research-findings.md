# 02. Research Findings

## 1. Investigation of Official Haramaya University Sources

Research was conducted across official Haramaya University web domains, direct directorate disclosures, and university statutory publications. The findings confirmed key institutional structures, roles, and administrative procedures.

### A. University Laboratories Management Directorate (ULMD)
- **Physical Headquarters:** Central Laboratory Building, Office Number 34, Main Campus, Dire Dawa, Ethiopia.
- **Directorate Leadership:** Dr. Meseret Girma (Director).
- **Institutional Mandate:** ULMD was established to support practical teaching, build research capacity, manage shared central instruments, and enforce quality and safety across all university laboratories.
- **Physical Scale:** Confirmed to oversee **more than 90 laboratories** (with operational scope expanding beyond 150 wet and dry laboratories across all campuses).
- **Core Units:** ULMD operates through five specialized organizational units:
  1. **Teaching-Learning Laboratories Unit:** Supervises academic laboratories; conducts quarterly/monthly inventory reviews; updates lab status through periodic surveys; evaluates laboratory rules.
  2. **Laboratory Technology Unit:** Provides technical and maintenance support; repairs all laboratory equipment; installs and calibrates newly purchased instruments; resolves internal laboratory electrical and plumbing issues.
  3. **Laboratory Materials and Consumable Unit:** Oversees resource utilization; evaluates purchase requisitions against existing inventories; tracks chemicals and supplies; collaborates with ICT to maintain database records; enforces Kaizen 5S principles.
  4. **Central Laboratories Unit:** Operates three high-end centralized facilities: (a) Soil, Water, and Plant Analysis Lab; (b) Food Microbiology and Toxicology Lab; (c) Biochemistry, Phytochemistry, and Instrumental Analysis Lab (housing AAS, HPLC, Soxhlet, deep freezers). Manages analytical service fees (charging 30% labor + 10% service for external clients; 20% labor with no service charge for MSc/PhD students).
  5. **Laboratory Animal Unit:** Oversees the breeding, care, and ethical housing of experimental animals.

### B. Estate Development and Facilities Management Directorate (EFMD)
- **Leadership:** Mr. Selahdin Ahmed (Director), Old IoT Building.
- **Structure:** Five core support processes, including **Building and Machineries Operations and Maintenance (BMOM)**.
- **Demarcation:** BMOM manages campus physical facilities, roads, building structures, and main electrical/water infrastructure. Internal laboratory instrument servicing is delineated to the ULMD Laboratory Technology Unit.

### C. Academic Colleges and Laboratories Footprint
- **Haramaya Institute of Technology (HiT):** 22+ specialized engineering laboratories (Reaction Engineering, HPLC, IC Engine, Highway, Geotechnical, CNC, Materials Testing, Grain Science).
- **College of Computing and Informatics (CCI):** High-density computing labs across Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, IT, and Information Science.
- **College of Agriculture and Environmental Sciences (CAES):** Plant, soil, feed, and seed testing laboratories.
- **College of Health and Medical Sciences (CHMS):** Clinical, microbiological, and public health labs at Harar.

## 2. International & Regional Laboratory Management Guidelines

To ensure the system adheres to recognized quality frameworks, external guidance was extracted from the **World Health Organization (WHO)** and the **Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC)**.

### A. WHO Laboratory Quality Stepwise Implementation (LQSI) Tool
- **Source:** WHO LQSI Toolkit (ISO 15189 Clauses 5.3.1.1, 5.3.1.7, 4.6.3; ISO 17025).
- **Mandatory Equipment Register Schema:** Every functional piece of laboratory equipment must be cataloged with:
  1. Unique Asset Label / Code (including laboratory prefix).
  2. Equipment Identity (generic name, brand, model).
  3. Manufacturer Serial Number.
  4. Manufacturer Name and Contact Information.
  5. Date of Purchase and Date Placed into Service (Commissioning).
  6. Exact Physical Location (Campus, Building, Room).
  7. Current Operational Condition (`Functioning properly`, `In need of maintenance`, `In need of calibration`, `Defective/Out of service`).
  8. Assigned Service Provider / Technical Custodian.
  9. Maintenance and Calibration Frequency (interval in days).
  10. Date of Last Maintenance/Calibration and Date of Next Scheduled Service.

### B. Africa CDC: Laboratory Equipment Management Guidance (August 2023)
- **Target Audience:** African Union Member States and institutional laboratory networks.
- **Key Recommendations:**
  1. **Lifecycle Governance:** Equipment management must encompass the complete lifecycle: Planning & Specification -> Incoming Inspection & Commissioning -> Inventory Tagging -> Preventive Maintenance -> Calibration -> Corrective Repair -> Condemnation/Disposal.
  2. **Dedicated Technical Bioengineers:** Establish centralized bioengineering calibration and maintenance facilities to service instruments within the network (directly mirrors the ULMD Laboratory Technology Unit structure).
  3. **Preventive Maintenance Schedules (PMS):** Routine servicing must be scheduled proactively rather than relying on reactive break-fix responses.
  4. **Equipment History Records:** Every piece of equipment must maintain an unbroken historical record of all repairs, replaced parts, calibration results, and physical movements.
