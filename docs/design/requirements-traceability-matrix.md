**Implementation boundary:** Stage 6 added same-transaction maintenance audit snapshots. User/security audit events and the read-only audit UI remain unimplemented. Stage 7 adds calibration audit events. DEC-02's request/decision mechanism is implemented with approver roles disabled by default; approval trigger, authorized approver designation/count, supporting evidence, and rejection/cancellation disposition remain stakeholder decisions. The closure policy for degraded/unrepairable outcomes also remains unresolved.

**Stage 7 boundary:** Calibration history and computed due/overdue views are implemented with immutable audit snapshots. Preventive-maintenance schedules and activities remain unimplemented because no approved schedule/activity entity, data dictionary, or workflow is defined. Failed-calibration disposition, reminder cadence, and the initial due-date baseline for never-calibrated assets remain stakeholder decisions.
